# Appendix B - Hard Business Rules (603 items)

Formulas, cutoffs, sequencing locks, status transitions and derivations that MUST be implemented exactly as stated. These are extracted verbatim-in-substance from the module specs.

## 01 Masters  (79 rules)

- FINANCIAL YEAR START: Start Date MUST be 1 April of a chosen year. Reject anything else.
- FINANCIAL YEAR END: End Date is DERIVED as 31 March of the following year and is not independently editable.
- FINANCIAL YEAR LABEL: DERIVED as the calendar year the year STARTS in. FY starting 01-Apr-2026 = label '2026' = 01-Apr-2026 to 31-Mar-2027.
- FINANCIAL YEAR NON-OVERLAP: Financial Years cannot overlap each other.
- FINANCIAL YEAR CURRENCY: Only ONE Financial Year may be marked Is Current at a time, and it is managed automatically from today's date - never typed.
- FINANCIAL YEAR LOCK IS ONE-WAY: Status may only move Open -> Locked, never back. No unlock exists anywhere in this version.
- THE LOCK RULE (absolute, overrides every role permission): Once a Financial Year is Locked, every field on that record AND every record dated inside that year IN EVERY MODULE becomes permanently read-only. Nothing dated inside it can be added, edited or deleted by anyone - not even the Super Admin who locked it. A locked year also freezes any Tax Head scoped to it.
- FINANCIAL YEAR DELETION: deletable outright only while NO record in ANY module references it. Once anything references it, Delete is disabled forever and Locking is the only remaining action.
- DELETE/DEACTIVATE IS SUPER ADMIN ONLY on every one of the 21 sub-masters, with no exception - including sub-masters other roles may add to and edit. Non-Super-Admin roles must not have the control RENDERED at all.
- MASTERS READ-THROUGH RULE: 'No access' hides the Masters admin screen only. It must NEVER stop the app reading Master values to populate dropdowns inside screens that role is allowed to use. Without this, Invoice, Expense, Payroll and Employee could not function for anyone but Super Admin.
- DERIVED FIELDS ARE READ-ONLY AND NEVER TYPED - standing rule across the whole module. Applies to: Financial Year Label, End Date, Is Current FY, Locked By, Locked On, Registered Address (Primary), Number Prefix Example Preview, Net Working Hours per Day, Saturday Override 'Pattern Says', Pay Cycle Resolved Periods, Email Template Name, Email Triggered From.
- NO TAX MAY EVER BE HARDCODED. The system must have no opinion about which taxes exist and must name none. There is NO built-in default tax anywhere in this CMS. An empty Taxes list must produce documents with no tax lines rather than erroring or falling back to a default.
- TAX NAME UNIQUENESS: Tax Name must be unique WITHIN A SCOPE (not globally).
- TAX RATE FIELD CONDITIONALITY: Rate (%) required iff Tax Type is GST-IGST or Percentage; CGST Rate and SGST Rate required iff Tax Type is GST-CGST+SGST; Fixed Amount required iff Tax Type is Fixed Amount; Declaration Cap required iff Tax Type is Declaration Cap.
- SGST MUST EQUAL CGST: on a GST - CGST + SGST head, SGST Rate must equal CGST Rate; each is half the total; the head prints as TWO lines, half each. GST - IGST prints as ONE combined line.
- TAX EFFECTIVITY WINDOW: a head applies from Applies From onward. Records dated BEFORE Applies From never see it. Blank Applies Until means still in force; setting it retires the head from that date forward WITHOUT touching any record already calculated under it.
- PRINT LABEL DEFAULT: if Print Label is blank, fall back to Tax Name.
- SLAB BAND VALIDATION: bands may not overlap and may not leave a gap between one band's To and the next band's From. Band From and Band To are both INCLUSIVE. EXACTLY ONE band may leave Band To blank ('and above') and it MUST be the last band. Validate INLINE as bands are entered, not only on save.
- SLAB APPLICATION FORMULA: Progressive = each band's rate applies only to the portion of the base inside that band, total = SUM across bands. Flat by Band = the single band the base lands in determines ONE rate applied to the ENTIRE base. These produce very different numbers, so the choice is asked, never assumed.
- SPLIT COMPONENT TOTAL: component shares must total EXACTLY 100% of the head's calculated amount. A head with zero component rows is a single undivided tax - the common case.
- RELIEF RULE CEILING: the relief applied is the LESSER of the configured Relief Amount and the tax actually computed, so a relief can NEVER turn into a refund. Relief Amount is required unless Relief Type = 'Reduce tax to zero'. Relief Rules apply to income-tax scopes only.
- TAX SCHEME DEFAULT: exactly ONE scheme may be Is Default. Employees who never choose are assessed under it.
- TAX SCHEME DISAPPEARANCE RULE: if no schemes are created, the entire concept vanishes from the interface - the Tax Scheme field on a tax head is hidden and My Tax Declarations shows no scheme choice at all. Zero, two or five schemes are all valid states.
- TAX ARITHMETIC LOCK (data-integrity lock, deliberately STRICTER than any role permission): once a head has been used in ANY calculation, its numbers become read-only FOR EVERYONE INCLUDING SUPER ADMIN - Rate, Amount, slab bands, split component shares, ceilings, thresholds, caps and Borne By. Only labels (Tax Name, Print Label, Notes) stay editable. An issued invoice or a processed payslip must ALWAYS be reproducible from the configuration that produced it.
- TAX RATE-CHANGE SEQUENCE (enforced, not suggested): NEVER edit an existing head to change a rate. Set the current head's Applies Until to the day BEFORE the change, then add a new head (usually via Duplicate) with the new figures and an Applies From date. Documents already issued keep resolving to the old head. Editing a locked figure must not be offered as an option at all; the tooltip on the disabled field explains the Duplicate route.
- TAX HEADS ARE NEVER HARD-DELETED once used on any Invoice, Expense, Purchase Bill, payslip or declaration - Active/Inactive toggle only, so historical records keep resolving to the head that produced them.
- SCOPE IS A FIXED LIST OF SEVEN. A new attachment point may become an eighth option; a new TAX never may, because a tax is a record, not an option.
- EXPENSE CLAIM TYPE ROUTING: Reimbursable is raisable by EVERY role and ends in a payout to the claimant, entering the reimbursement queue (Module 12, Sections 2.1-2.2). Company-Paid is raisable ONLY by Super Admin and Accountant, is settled from a company bank account, never enters the reimbursement queue, and lands in Company Expenses (Module 12, Section 2.4). An employee must never see a Company-Paid category in their dropdown.
- CLAIM TYPE CHANGE IS FORWARD-ONLY: changing Claim Type on a type already in use must warn explicitly, NAME the count of records already using it, leave those records exactly where they are, and apply from that point forward - NEVER retroactively.
- LEAVE CARRY-FORWARD CAP: Max Carry Forward Days must be LESS THAN OR EQUAL TO the Default Annual Quota, and is required only when Carry Forward Allowed = Yes.
- LEAVE QUOTA ZERO IS VALID: Default Annual Quota of 0 is allowed, for types like Work From Home that do not consume a quota.
- LEAVE PAY LINKAGE: the Paid/Unpaid dropdown DRIVES whether Payroll deducts pay for that leave type.
- HOLIDAY DATE SCOPE: a Holiday's Date must fall inside its linked Financial Year's date range.
- HOLIDAY DELETION IS A PARTIAL EXCEPTION: a Holiday never used in a COMPLETED Payroll run may be deleted outright by Super Admin. Once a Payroll run has been CALCULATED for a period containing that holiday's date, it switches to the standard rule - Active/Inactive only, no hard delete.
- HOLIDAY EFFECT ON PAY: working-day and payable-day calculations EXCLUDE Mandatory holidays. An employee taking an OPTIONAL holiday draws from their leave balance.
- COMPANY PROFILE IS A SINGLETON: exactly one record, created during initial setup, editable forever, never deleted or deactivated. It has no Active/Inactive status field - protection comes entirely from Role Access.
- PRIMARY ADDRESS SINGLETON: only one address may be Primary; marking a new one automatically un-marks the previous. Company Profile's 'Registered Address (Primary)' is derived from it.
- PIN CODE: exactly 6 digits. WEBSITE: must start with http:// or https://. PHONE: 10 digits. YEAR OF INCORPORATION: 4-digit year.
- NUMBERING FORMAT: {PREFIX}-{FY_LABEL}-{RUNNING_NUMBER}. Prefix 2-6 UPPERCASE letters, no spaces. Digits between 3 and 6.
- STARTING NUMBER IMMUTABILITY: the Starting Number for a code type CANNOT be changed once any code of that type has been generated. Only the prefix and future behaviour may change going forward.
- FY RESET BEHAVIOUR: when Resets Every FY = Yes, the running number restarts at the Starting Number on the FIRST record created in each new financial year.
- NUMBERING CHANGES ARE FORWARD-ONLY: changing a code type that has already generated records affects only new records; existing codes are never rewritten. Require a confirmation pop-up naming the existing count.
- BANK ACCOUNT NUMBER: numeric, plausible length 9-18 digits. Confirm Account Number must match EXACTLY and is not stored separately. IFSC: EXACTLY 11 characters - 4 letters, then '0', then 6 alphanumeric characters.
- ONE DEFAULT BANK ACCOUNT AT A TIME. The Default account cannot be deactivated until a new Default is chosen - block with 'This is your default account - please choose a new default before deactivating this one.'
- ONLY ACTIVE BANK ACCOUNTS ARE EVER OFFERED in any dropdown, across all nine consumer screens.
- DEACTIVATION NEVER REWRITES HISTORY: a deactivated bank account disappears from every dropdown but rows already recorded against it keep showing it. A payment that genuinely went to a now-closed account still went there; a record that quietly forgot which account it used would be worse than one naming a closed one. The same principle applies to every deactivated Master entry.
- HSN/SAC LINKED TAX IS LIVE, NEVER FIXED: the dropdown is built at render time from whatever in-force Sales/Billing or Purchase scope heads currently exist in Taxes. A head retired or re-versioned in Taxes is reflected here immediately with no separate update. To change what a code charges, edit the TAX HEAD - never the code row.
- HSN/SAC CUSTOM RATE: Custom GST Type and Custom GST Rate are required iff Linked Tax = '- Custom -', and are NOT saved as a reusable head.
- WORK WEEK CORE FORMULA: Net Working Hours per Day = Gross Hours per Day MINUS Unpaid Break (hours). DERIVED, never typed. This is THE ONLY figure Payroll compares against. Company's current answer: 9 - 1 = 8.
- WEEKLY TARGET: hours per week EXCLUDING Saturdays. Company's current answer: 40. Payroll checks the MONTHLY total; the weekly figure is what people quote.
- EXACTLY ONE COMPANY-WIDE WORK WEEK POLICY MAY BE ACTIVE AT A TIME, and it is the fallback. A NARROWER POLICY ALWAYS WINS over the company-wide default for the people it names.
- DAY-STATUS BAND RESOLUTION: hours at or above the Net target = Full Day; hours inside the Grace Window of the target = Full Day (grace); hours at or above the Half Day Threshold but under the target = Half Day; hours below the Half Day Threshold = Absent. Grace Window of 0 switches grace off.
- SATURDAY PATTERN: resolved for all 52 Saturdays of the active Financial Year from the policy's Saturday Rule. Company's current answer: odd Saturdays (1st, 3rd, 5th) working, even Saturdays (2nd, 4th) off.
- COLLISION PRECEDENCE ON A SATURDAY: a MANDATORY holiday ALWAYS overrides the Saturday rule when 'Mandatory Holiday Overrides Saturday' is on - the Saturday becomes non-working and required hours drop to ZERO (worked example: 15-08-2026 is a 3rd Saturday the odd pattern would make working; Independence Day makes it non-working). An OPTIONAL holiday NEVER overrides - the Saturday stays working and anyone taking the day draws on their leave balance. A dated Saturday Override row takes precedence over the pattern.
- WORKING-SATURDAY HOURS: Full day = the Net figure; Half day = 50% of Net; or the explicit Custom Saturday Hours value, which is required only when 'Hours on a Working Saturday' = Custom hours.
- SATURDAY OVERRIDE REASON IS MANDATORY AND NOT NEGOTIABLE, and displays permanently against that date on the Saturday Calendar. A Saturday that silently changes pay for the whole company needs a stated cause.
- COMPENSATORY MAKE-UP RULE (the core rule): someone who leaves halfway through a day does NOT simply lose half a day's pay - they work the missing hours on another day OF THEIR OWN CHOOSING and the shortfall clears. ONLY HOURS NEVER MADE UP reach the payslip.
- COMPENSATION WINDOW: the make-up day must fall within Compensation Window (days) of the shortfall. 0 means the SAME PAYROLL PERIOD ONLY.
- COMPENSATION CAP: Max Compensatory Hours per Month is a ceiling so compensation cannot quietly become unpaid overtime. 0 removes the cap.
- MAKE-UP DAY ELIGIBILITY: a week off is permitted only if 'Allow Make-Up on a Week Off' is on; a mandatory holiday only if 'Allow Make-Up on a Mandatory Holiday' is on, which is OFF BY DEFAULT because a mandatory holiday should stay a holiday.
- UNCOMPENSATED SHORTFALL CONVERSION: per the policy's 'Uncompensated Shortfall Becomes' - Pro-rata per hour (Loss of Pay) [DEFAULT, the fairest], Rounded to nearest half day, Rounded up to a full day, Deduct from leave balance first then Loss of Pay, or Ignore (no payroll impact).
- EXCESS HOURS CONVERSION: per 'Excess Hours Become' - Compensatory credit that expires with the window, Comp Off leave balance, Paid overtime, or Nothing (not tracked). With 'Count Overtime Beyond Net' on, hours past the daily target are banked and can offset a shortfall elsewhere IN THE SAME PERIOD.
- WORK WEEK POLICY CHANGES ARE FORWARD-ONLY: a policy change never rewrites a payroll period that is already Processed - which is exactly why Effective From exists. Deactivating a policy leaves every already-Processed period untouched and applies only to future runs.
- A COMPANY-WIDE WORK WEEK POLICY CANNOT BE DEACTIVATED WHILE IT IS THE ONLY ACTIVE ONE, since that would leave employees with no target to measure against.
- PAY CYCLE IS NOT A CALENDAR MONTH. Cycle Type = Calendar Month runs the 1st to the last day and IGNORES Start Day and End Day. Cycle Type = Fixed Cycle uses Start Day (day of the PREVIOUS month the period opens) and End Day (day of the CURRENT month it closes). Company's current answer: 26th to 25th.
- END DAY IS THE CUTOFF every downstream calculation reads. SALARY CREDIT DAY is a SEPARATE field for when money actually lands, and must always be later. Company's current answer: credit on the 30th, five days after the End Day of 25.
- SALARY CREDIT DAY CLAMPING: a Credit Day of 30 or 31 must be clamped automatically to the real last day of a shorter month, so it still resolves INSIDE February rather than silently rolling into March.
- THE CUTOFF RECONCILIATION RULE, STATED PRECISELY: any event with a date falling AFTER the End Day belongs to the NEXT pay period, NEVER the one that just closed. Worked example on the 26th-25th cycle: one day of Approved Casual Leave on 27 July 2026 is two days past the July cutoff (period 26 June - 25 July), so July's payslip does NOT include it; it is picked up automatically by the August 2026 period (26 July - 25 August) and prints there instead. It applies UNIFORMLY to EVERY dated input Payroll reads - attendance corrections, compensatory make-up days, ad-hoc adjustments - not leave specifically. Nothing needs a person to remember to move it; THE DATE ALONE DECIDES which run picks it up.
- PAY CYCLE CHANGES ARE FORWARD-ONLY: a change never rewrites a period that has already run; it only changes what date range and credit date the NEXT Run Payroll resolves for a given month.
- EMAIL MASTER SWITCH: 'Sending Enabled' off stops EVERY outbound email at once without touching a single template. The in-app notification bell is unaffected either way, because it does not depend on email delivery.
- EMAIL TEMPLATE ENABLE SEMANTICS: switching a template off silences ONLY that email. The in-app notification for the same event STILL FIRES. Enabled here never suppresses in-app awareness of the underlying event, only its email copy.
- EMAIL PROVIDER CONDITIONALITY: Custom SMTP requires SMTP Host, SMTP Port and Encryption. A named provider (SendGrid / Amazon SES / Mailgun / Postmark) REPLACES those three with a single API Key field.
- CREDENTIAL MASKING CONVENTION: the SMTP Password (and API Key) is never shown in full once saved - the same convention used for every stored credential in this CMS.
- FROM ADDRESS AUTHORISATION: From Email Address must be a domain the configured server is authorised to send as, or messages are likely to land in spam or be rejected outright.
- EMAIL TEMPLATE ROWS ARE NEVER DELETED once the trigger exists. Enabled/Disabled is the only lifecycle, because disabling a template that later needs re-enabling should not require re-creating it from scratch. Server Settings has no add/delete, only edit.
- WHAT CAUSES AN EMAIL TO FIRE is defined at its trigger point in the owning module, NOT in Email Configuration. This screen controls only WHETHER it sends and WHAT IT SAYS.
- STATUS FILTER DEFAULT ON EVERY SUB-MASTER LIST: defaults to Active, with an explicit 'include inactive' option. Retired entries are hidden by default but NEVER silently unreachable.
- EXPORT FIDELITY: every export contains EXACTLY the filtered set, with a header stating the filters applied.
- FILTER PARITY: every role that can open a sub-master tab gets that tab's FULL filter set and export - never a reduced version.

## 02 Employee  (54 rules)

- NUMBERING: Employee ID is auto-generated on save as EMP-<Financial Year>-<4-digit running number>, e.g. EMP-2026-0001. Must be added to Masters → Company Profile → Number Prefixes (part e) for Super Admin to configure. Asset Tags referenced from Asset Management follow AST-<FY>-<4-digit>, e.g. AST-2026-0001.
- UNIQUENESS: Email must be unique across all employees, because it is also the login identifier. Login Email is read-only and mirrors the Personal Details Email — there is no separate username field.
- VALIDATION — Date of Birth cannot be in the future; the employee must be at least 18 years old as of the Joining Date.
- VALIDATION — Joining Date cannot be in the future.
- VALIDATION — First Name 2–50 characters; Last Name up to 50 characters; Phone and Contact Phone must be 10-digit Indian mobile numbers.
- VALIDATION — IFSC Code is exactly 11 characters: 4 letters, then '0', then 6 alphanumeric characters.
- VALIDATION — PAN is exactly 10 characters: 5 letters, 4 digits, 1 letter (e.g. AAAAA9999A).
- VALIDATION — Aadhaar Number is 12 digits and is DISPLAYED MASKED (e.g. XXXX-XXXX-1234) once saved.
- VALIDATION — Confirm Bank Account Number must match Bank Account Number exactly and is NOT stored separately.
- VALIDATION — Reporting Manager cannot be the employee themselves, and the system BLOCKS any selection that would create a circular reporting chain (A reports to B, B reports to A).
- DEFAULT — Employment Status defaults to Active on a new record (a status must always have a value for approvals, payroll etc. to work).
- DEFAULT — Create Login defaults to No. Account Status defaults to Enabled once a login is created.
- FORMULA — Gross Monthly = Basic + DA + HRA + LTA (only if paid monthly) + Special Allowance + Other Earnings. Variable/Performance Pay is EXCLUDED from Gross Monthly.
- FORMULA — Net Monthly (before income tax) = Gross Monthly − every employee-borne statutory line in the Statutory Deductions & Contributions block that is currently Applicable − Insurance Employee Contribution. Income tax is calculated separately and monthly by Payroll (Module 6), never here.
- FORMULA — Annual CTC = (Gross Monthly × 12) + Variable/Performance Pay (annualized) + (every company-borne statutory line whose head has 'Include in Cost to Company' = Yes, × 12) + (Insurance Employer Contribution × 12).
- FORMULA — Leave Remaining = Allowed + Carried Forward − Taken − Pending. Carried Forward is calculated automatically at year-end and is never typed in.
- FORMULA — Attendance: Gross Hours = Check-Out − Check-In; Net Worked Hours = Gross Hours − Break; Balance = Net Worked Hours vs Required Hours for that date (from Masters Section 3.20). Day Status (Full Day / Full Day (grace) / Half Day / Absent) is DERIVED from hours, not from presence. Example given: nine hours on site with a two-hour lunch is a shortfall.
- COMPLIANCE VALIDATION (Code on Wages, 2019) — Basic Salary + Dearness Allowance must be AT LEAST 50% of Annual CTC. The four Labour Codes were notified effective November 21, 2025, with enforcement phasing in through 2026. Saving a structure that fails this check must WARN and IDEALLY BLOCK, rather than silently accept a non-compliant split — the real-world consequence is a shortfall on every statutory contribution calculated on Basic or Basic + DA across every affected employee, discovered later, with back-payment and penalties. Confirm the exact enforcement date and whether 'warn' or 'hard block' is right before build, since labour-code rollout was still being finalized state by state.
- HARD BLOCK — Leave: if days requested exceed Remaining for that Leave Type, submission is BLOCKED OUTRIGHT with the exact shortfall shown (e.g. 'You have 2 days remaining for Casual Leave, but requested 4'). THERE IS NO SUBMIT-ANYWAY PATH and no Manager override. Separately, a request overlapping dates with an existing Pending or Approved leave is also blocked. To Date must be on or after From Date.
- HARD BLOCK — Tax declaration amounts exceeding the head's 'Maximum Amount per Period' show the cap inline and BLOCK Submit rather than silently trimming the figure.
- SEQUENCING LOCK — Exit checklist order is a RULE, not a suggestion. Step 1 Generate resignation acceptance letter (nothing else can be actioned until it exists) → Step 2 Return ALL assigned assets (locked until 1; checks off only when EVERY asset shows Returned or Retired) → Steps 3, 4 and 5 (Mark final settlement due / Disable login / Change status to Exited) are ALL locked until step 2 is done.
- NO PARTIAL EXIT — there is no 'returned most of them' state. The asset step completes only when the outstanding count is ZERO. An asset written off as lost must be EXPLICITLY marked Retired in Asset Management by someone with the rights to do that — a deliberate decision with a name against it, never a checklist item quietly ticked.
- STATUS TRANSITION — Employment Status: Active / Inactive / On Notice / Exited. On exit, status becomes Exited and an Exit Date is recorded, defaulting to the Last Working Day stated on the letter. After exit the employee cannot log in, but historical payslips, tickets, project history and documents remain intact and viewable by HR, Accountant and Super Admin.
- STATUS TRANSITION — Document: Pending Verification → Verified OR Rejected. Every new upload starts at Pending Verification. Rejecting REQUIRES a short reason before it can be confirmed.
- STATUS TRANSITION — Tax Declaration: Draft (not used in the tax calculation) → Submitted (LOCKED for the financial year, used in Payroll's calculation from the next run onward) → Verified (proofs checked). The calculation uses SUBMITTED figures all year regardless of Verified status — Verified is an audit trail and possible year-end reconciliation, NOT a blocking gate on Payroll.
- STATUS TRANSITION — Profile Change Request: Pending → Approved or Rejected. While Pending, the OLD VALUE STAYS IN EFFECT and keeps displaying with a 'Pending Approval' badge, and the employee CANNOT submit another change to the same field.
- STATUS TRANSITION — Asset Request: Pending / Approved / Rejected / Completed. Routed to the employee's Reporting Manager; Super Admin can also act on any request.
- STATUS TRANSITION — Ticket (employee-side): an employee can move a ticket between Open, In Progress and Resolved. Moving a ticket to CLOSED is reserved for whoever confirms resolution on the requester's side, per the Ticket module.
- STATUS TRANSITION — Task (employee-side): limited to To Do / In Progress / In Review / Done. Employees cannot delete a task or manage sprints.
- DELETE RULE — an employee can delete their own document ONLY while it is still Pending Verification. Once HR has Verified or Rejected it, only HR can remove it — this keeps a clean record of what was actually reviewed. Deleting shows a confirm-before-you-go prompt.
- PASSWORD RULE — HR NEVER sees or relays any password. Turning Create Login on generates a random temporary password immediately, emails it to the employee's registered address as part of the welcome email, and sets Must Change Password on Next Login = Yes. Reset Password does exactly the same. Logging in with a temporary password redirects MANDATORILY to the Change Own Password form (Current / New / Confirm) before anything else in the system is reachable; on save the flag flips to No.
- SALARY STRUCTURE VERSIONING — entering a new structure with a new Effective From date (promotion, hike, correction) AUTOMATICALLY closes out the previous one into Structure Change History rather than overwriting it. Effective From cannot be in the future beyond the current financial year.
- CONFIGURATION-DRIVEN, NEVER HARDCODED — no tax is named anywhere in this module. The Statutory Deductions & Contributions block, the Statutory Identifiers block, and the entire My Tax Declarations form are all generated from Masters → Taxes (Section 3.2). Rates, ceilings, thresholds, slabs and cut-offs are NEVER entered on the employee record and never appear in this spec; they belong to the tax head. This module only displays the RESULT of applying a head to one person's pay.
- EMPTY-BY-DESIGN — on a fresh installation with no tax heads configured, an employee's Salary Structure shows earnings, Gross Monthly, Net Monthly and Annual CTC with NO statutory lines at all, and Net Monthly EQUALS Gross Monthly. That is CORRECT BEHAVIOUR, not a broken screen. The block's empty-state text reads exactly: 'No statutory deductions or contributions are configured. Add them in Masters → Taxes.'
- AUTOMATIC HEAD FILTERING — a statutory head appears on an employee's structure only if it is in force on the Effective From date AND matches that employee's location, employment type and tax scheme. A head restricted to certain office locations only appears for employees at those locations. This replaces the old state-slab lookup written specifically for Professional Tax; the mechanism is now general and no module knows the name of any tax that uses it.
- APPLICABLE-TOGGLE CONSISTENCY — a per-employee Applicable decision flows into this month's payslip, every future payslip, and the Offer & Appointment Letter, rather than being excluded by hand in three places. Editable by Super Admin OR Accountant, and by no one else.
- INSURANCE PREMIUMS ARE NEVER ENTERED TWICE — the selected plan's Company Contribution and Employee Contribution (Masters Section 3.19) flow automatically into Annual CTC and Net Monthly. Insurance stays a NAMED line (a benefit premium, not a tax) rather than joining the statutory block.
- CUSTOM TERMS AUTO-QUEUE — each Custom Term on an Offer/Appointment Letter is queued automatically on save as an Ad-hoc Payslip Adjustment (Payroll Section 2.1a) against that employee, effective from their Joining Date. If the record isn't Active yet the adjustment sits queued and picks up automatically the first time payroll runs for them after joining. No manual re-entry in Payroll.
- LETTER TRUTHFULNESS — the letter's Statutory Components Disclosed section lists whichever statutory heads will ACTUALLY apply to this person, by the name Super Admin gave each in Masters, matching exactly what the Salary Structure tab shows, so an employee never receives a letter promising something different from what their payslip later shows. If no statutory heads apply, the section is OMITTED entirely rather than printing an empty heading.
- LEAVE ALLOCATION — once a record is saved as Active within a financial year, the system allocates the Default Annual Quota for every Active Leave Type from Masters, PRORATED by Joining Date. Fully automatic; the onboarding checklist shows it as a confirmation only.
- ATTENDANCE RECORDS HOURS, NOT PRESENCE — only Check-In, Check-Out and Break are typed; every pay-affecting figure is derived from the Work Week & Hours policy (Masters Section 3.20). The form shows the shortfall BEFORE it is saved, along with how it may be made up (Leave Module 5 Section 2.6).
- ATTENDANCE WRITE PATH — manual entry and the future biometric feed write to the SAME underlying attendance record. Turning Biometric from 'Coming Soon' to active changes only the Recorded By source, which is precisely why Recorded By holds either a person or a device/system source rather than being a strict user reference, and requires no restructuring of the tab or of anything downstream in Payroll. Today, only Super Admin can manually record check-in/check-out.
- CONFIRM-BEFORE-YOU-GO PROMPTS are required on: disabling a login, removing login access entirely (a STRONGER confirmation than a simple disable), deleting a Pending document, rejecting a document, rejecting a change request (since a reason must be entered), and both irreversible-feeling exit actions (disable login, mark Exited).
- FILTER & EXPORT CONVENTION — every list screen in this module carries a documented filter set plus filter-based export. Filters are available to EVERY role that can open the screen and narrow what a role already sees, never widen it. Super Admin gets every filter on every page plus Created By / Last Modified By and a 'show exited employees' toggle on the Directory. Export ALWAYS follows the current filter/search/sort set, with a header block stating which filters produced it. Baseline set (search, date range, status, owner, active/inactive) is inherited from RBAC Module 14, Section 2.2.
- EXPORT EXCEPTIONS — My Payslips has no Employee filter because every row is already theirs (convention exception b.1); Super Admin's equivalent view does have one. Personal notifications are filterable but deliberately NOT exportable. The header notification popover stays a simple recent list with no filter controls. My Documents export exports the LIST (type, status, dates), not the uploaded files.
- DIRECTORY DEFAULTS — 25 rows per page, page-size selector 10/25/50/100. Sort options: Name A→Z / Z→A, Joining Date Newest/Oldest, Department. Search covers Name, Employee ID, Email, Phone.
- UI CONVENTION — table view for admin-facing lists (Employee Directory, HR Document Verification Queue); card/dashboard style for self-service screens (My Profile, My Leave, My Payslips, My Documents, My Tickets, My Projects & Tasks, My Assets, Dashboard), favouring summary cards and short lists over dense tables. Every self-service screen must be MOBILE-FRIENDLY BY DEFAULT — laid out cleanly on a small screen, not a shrunk desktop table.
- EMPTY STATES (exact strings): 'No employees added yet.' / 'No documents uploaded yet.' / 'No assets assigned to you yet.' / 'No tickets assigned to you right now.' / "You're not assigned to any projects yet." / 'No tasks assigned to you.' / 'No notifications yet.' / 'No announcements right now.' / 'Not enrolled in any insurance plan yet.' / 'No letter generated yet for this employee.' / 'No statutory deductions or contributions are configured. Add them in Masters → Taxes.' / 'There are no declarations to make. Your employer has not configured any deductions that reduce taxable income.' / 'No tax declaration submitted yet for this financial year.' / Dashboard widget examples: 'No open tickets — nice and clear', 'No announcements right now.'
- EDIT MODEL — the Employee Master Record opens in READ mode; a single Edit unlocks Personal Details, Employment Details, Bank & Statutory, Salary Structure, Insurance & Benefits and Emergency Contact TOGETHER with one Save Changes committing all at once and one Cancel discarding. Account & Login actions and Document verification actions are EXCLUDED from this flow — they are immediate and individually confirmed.
- DERIVED VALUES ARE NEVER TYPED — every field flagged derived (Gross Monthly, Net Monthly, Annual CTC, Insurance Employee Contribution, Login Email, Must Change Password on Next Login, Last Login, Carried Forward, all attendance derivations, Structure Change History rows, Statutory blocks) is rendered READ-ONLY.
- DO NOT BUILD — Team Payslips. The screen was removed from this module entirely per explicit instruction and no longer exists in the prototype. A Manager has NO payslip-related visibility into their team at all. The 'Show Salary Amount to Manager' toggle is retired on this module's side.
- DO NOT BUILD — ESOPs (deliberately excluded, would be its own module), a candidate-tracking system, and any named tax head. Biometric Attendance and Timesheets/Milestones are 'Coming Soon' placeholders only: Biometric is visible but disabled and not clickable; Milestones and Timesheets are placeholder tabs holding their eventual layout position.
- OUT OF SCOPE — this module has nothing to do with Meshrd's retail hardware products or product billing; that side of the business runs on a separate website and is out of scope everywhere in this document.
- READ-ONLY BORROWED DATA — leave balances come from Leave, payslip amounts from Payroll, task lists from Project, ticket lists from Ticket, assigned-asset lists from Asset Management. This module only describes what an Employee or Manager sees and can do with that data from INSIDE the Employee module; it does not redesign, compute or edit any of it.

## 14 Roles & Access (RBAC)  (54 rules)

- NOTHING IS PERMANENTLY FIXED. Access is a setting everywhere in this system, never a hardcoded rule. Super Admin can take any screen from any module and show it to a role that would not normally see it - including a module whose default is no access at all - and can equally hide a screen a role currently has.
- RBAC IS SUPER ADMIN ONLY. Employee, Manager, Accountant and HR have no access whatsoever - not read-only, not partial. The module does not exist for them. The single exception is the 'Give HR Read-Only Access to the Permission Matrix' toggle (OFF by default), which grants HR view-only access to the permission matrix and Permission Change Log for compliance/audit, with no change rights.
- RBAC CANNOT BE OVERRIDDEN PER PERSON. Super Admin cannot be 'overridden' (they already have full access) and no other role gets individual exceptions into a module that is entirely closed to their role by design. The override mechanism this module IMPLEMENTS is a different thing from whether this module can itself be selectively opened for one person - it cannot, beyond the single HR-wide toggle.
- LAYER SEQUENCING LOCK: a screen must be VISIBLE (Layer 1) before Layers 2, 3 and 4 mean anything. On the Matrix, if a screen is Hidden the Visible/Hidden toggle is the ONLY control shown for it.
- HIDDEN MEANS ABSENT: a Hidden screen does not appear anywhere in that role's navigation - no greyed-out menu item, and no 'access denied' page reachable by URL guessing. It simply is not there.
- LAYER 2 ACTION LISTS ARE NEVER GENERIC. Each screen's real, documented action set populates this layer for that screen. There is no universal action list invented by RBAC and forced onto every screen.
- HARD ACTION EXCEPTIONS THAT ARE NOT TOGGLE-ABLE: Asset Master Record Delete/Deactivate is reserved for Super Admin only, never a toggle-able action for any other role. New/Edit Invoice locks EVERY field automatically once Status reaches Paid, regardless of role. Masters -> Taxes Delete and Deactivate stay Super Admin only regardless of the Accountant toggle, and NO ROLE can alter a tax head already used in a calculation.
- LAYER 3 IS INDEPENDENT OF LAYER 1: a field-level sensitivity toggle shows or hides just that field independent of whether the screen itself is Visible.
- LAYER 4 IS OMITTED WHERE MEANINGLESS: the All/Team/Own scope selector does not appear at all on a screen with no meaningful scope dimension (e.g. Masters Financial Year, which is inherently company-wide and singular), rather than being forced onto every screen.
- THE MATRIX SAVES IN BATCH: toggles accumulate as pending edits; a single explicit Save Changes commits all of them together, Discard Changes drops them. No instant-apply.
- PREVIEW USES SAVED STATE ONLY: 'Preview as this role' reflects currently SAVED settings, never unsaved draft changes still open on the Matrix, and must be unmistakably marked as preview mode via a persistent banner or border.
- HIDING A SCREEN IN USE SHOWS A WARNING BEFORE SAVE, not a silent removal and not a silent failure.
- OVERRIDES ARE VISUALLY DISTINCT: there must be a clear, consistent visual difference between 'role default' and 'person-specific override' everywhere both appear - on the Matrix (once a person's overrides diverge from their role) and especially on Individual Permission Overrides (4.3), where every override row is explicitly marked as different from the default, never blended in as if it were normal.
- THE PERMISSION CHANGE LOG IS READ-ONLY. Nothing on it is editable; it records what already happened.
- THE MATRIX IS ORGANIZED BY SPEC MODULE, NOT BY SIDEBAR POSITION. Sidebar navigation and the spec module tree were deliberately allowed to diverge on 2026-07-30 (Leave + Payroll share one 'Leave & Payroll' tab with My Tax Declarations; My Tickets moved under Ticket; My Projects & Tasks moved under Project). None of that is a Layer 1 visibility change and none of it touches the Matrix, which stays organized by spec module so entries remain auditable against the Screen Inventory.
- FILTER & EXPORT CONVENTION IS NOT OPTIONAL. It is a fixed platform convention, never a per-module toggle, and does not appear on the Role Permission Matrix as something to turn on or off.
- EVERY LIST SCREEN GETS FILTERS AND BOTH EXPORTS, FOR EVERY ROLE THAT CAN OPEN IT. There is no such thing as a filter that exists for one role and not another on the same screen. Export to PDF and Export to Excel are NOT a Super Admin privilege.
- SELF-SERVICE SCREENS ARE LIST SCREENS. My Leave, My Payslips, My Tickets, My Assets, My Documents, My Expenses, My Projects & Tasks and every other 'my own records' screen gets the full filter and export treatment.
- RECORD-DETAIL SCREENS ARE NOT LIST SCREENS. They have their own single-record download where relevant (payslip PDF, invoice PDF), which is a different thing from exporting a filtered list.
- ONLY TWO FILTER EXCEPTIONS, both about meaninglessness not restriction: (i) a dimension invisible to that role because every row already belongs to them (an Employee has no 'Employee' filter on their own payslip list); (ii) a filter on a field that role cannot see at all (Purchase Value hidden from Manager on the Asset Directory takes its range filter with it). The field's visibility decides; the filter follows.
- SCOPE BEFORE FILTER, ALWAYS. Role scope limits the data set before any filter is applied. Filters operate strictly inside that set, never outside it. A FILTER IS A LENS, NOT A KEY. This is what makes universal filter access and universal export access safe.
- FILTERS COMBINE WITH AND. Applied filters render as removable chips above the table with a 'Clear all' control.
- FILTER STATE PERSISTS WITHIN A SESSION ONLY, per user per screen - navigating into a record and back does not reset it - and does NOT persist across sessions.
- EMPTY RESULTS MUST NAME THE CAUSE: e.g. 'No tickets match Status = Closed and Date = This Month', with the clear-all control right there. A bare 'No records found' is not acceptable on a filtered screen.
- RESULT COUNT IS ALWAYS SHOWN in the form 'Showing 24 of 312'.
- STATUS FILTERS ARE MULTI-SELECT, never single-select.
- ACTIVE/INACTIVE FILTERS DEFAULT TO ACTIVE ONLY, with an explicit 'include inactive' option - never silently hide retired entries.
- FINANCIAL-YEAR PRESETS READ MASTERS -> FINANCIAL YEAR (Section 3.1). Never hardcode a date.
- COLUMN ALIGNMENT RULE (set 2026-07-31, replacing a briefly-held right-aligned rule): MONEY - any currency amount (totals, balances, rates in Rs., salaries, purchase and current values, line totals, deductions, net pay) is LEFT-ALIGNED in BOTH the header cell and the body cells, on every list screen, every report, every record tab, the invoice/bill line-item editor, and the Subtotal / Tax / Grand Total block. Rationale: currency amounts read from the left, aligned with the column heading above them and with the text columns beside them, so a table scans as one continuous left edge; money is shown with the Rs. symbol LEADING and a leading symbol is what the eye anchors on - right-aligning pushes that anchor to a different horizontal position on every row.
- COLUMN ALIGNMENT RULE: NON-MONEY FIGURES STAY RIGHT-ALIGNED - counts, quantities, hours, days, percentages, ageing in days. They are compared for magnitude rather than read as labels and carry no leading symbol. A single table can legitimately contain both, as the payroll and attendance screens do.
- COLUMN ALIGNMENT RULE: TEXT IS LEFT - names, codes, statuses, dates, identifiers.
- COLUMN ALIGNMENT RULE: THE HEADER CELL FOLLOWS THE BODY CELLS. A left-aligned column of amounts under a right-aligned header reads as a mistake. Header and body are always set together.
- COLUMN ALIGNMENT RULE: IDENTIFIERS THAT HAPPEN TO BE NUMERIC ARE TEXT, NOT FIGURES - HSN/SAC codes, PIN codes, invoice numbers, phone numbers, '30 days' payment terms. Left-aligned, like any other label.
- COLUMN ALIGNMENT RULE: A MASKED AMOUNT KEEPS THE ALIGNMENT OF THE AMOUNT IT REPLACES. Where a field-level permission hides a figure from a role, the placeholder sits in the same column alignment, so masking never shifts the layout for that role.
- COLUMN ALIGNMENT IMPLEMENTATION: enforced in ONE place, not per screen. Every table builder normalises its money columns through a single shared helper, so a new screen inherits the rule automatically and cannot drift. Changing the house rule is a one-line change in that helper, not a sweep across every column definition - which is precisely how the earlier inconsistency arose.
- EXPORT EXPORTS EXACTLY WHAT IS ON SCREEN: current filters, current search, current sort, current column set. THERE IS NO 'EXPORT EVERYTHING' OPTION that bypasses the applied filter - a user who wants everything clears the filters first, which is a visible, deliberate act.
- EXPORT RESPECTS ROLE SCOPE IDENTICALLY. Export is never a backdoor to more data than the screen itself shows. THE EXPORT MATCHES THE SCREEN, SO A VISIBILITY FLAG NEVER NEEDS A SEPARATE EXPORT RULE.
- EVERY EXPORTED FILE CARRIES A HEADER BLOCK listing the filters that produced it, the result count, who exported it, and when. A spreadsheet of 24 rows that does not say it was filtered to one month is actively misleading once emailed.
- EXPORT FORMAT: PDF uses the same letterhead treatment as other generated documents in this CMS (invoices, payslips, salary certificates), drawn from Company Profile (Masters, Section 3.6). Excel is a plain data export - one row per record, columns matching the screen - NOT a formatted report.
- SETTINGS DEFAULTS: Theme = Light; Accent Colour = Blue (of six presets: Blue, Purple, Green, Amber, Rose, Teal); Display Scale = 110% (presets 90/100/110/125/150% plus a free slider 80-200%). 110% is the default because that is the size the prototype was reviewed and signed off at; 100% remains the design baseline the type scale is specified in.
- SETTINGS ARE SESSION-ONLY: theme, accent and display scale are held in memory for the session only; NOTHING is written to browser storage, consistent with the filter-persistence rule in the System tab and this prototype's rule of never persisting outside the session.
- CUSTOM ACCENT DERIVATION FORMULA: the picker takes ONE colour and DERIVES three more - hover shade, subtle tinted-background fill, hairline border - by mixing toward WHITE on Light and toward THE DARK CANVAS on Dark, using the same relationships the six hand-tuned presets already use. The user never picks four colours twice, and a custom accent stays legible when the theme is switched. The interface recolours WHILE THE USER DRAGS the picker, not only on release.
- DISPLAY SCALE IS AN APPLICATION SETTING, NOT BROWSER ZOOM. Browser zoom is per-site and per-profile: it resets on a cleared profile, does not follow the user to another machine, and is invisible to the application. A display scale belonging to the user account means a shared workstation or a fresh browser still opens the CMS at the size that person works in, and it replaces per-screen breakpoints for large and high-density displays - one control applied once.
- SETTINGS ACCESS: the Appearance tab is open to all five roles; Regional & Financial and System tabs are Super Admin only. Settings is reached from the fixed link at the bottom-left of the sidebar AND from the profile menu - deliberately NOT from the module tree, which would list the same screen twice at once.
- REJECTION REQUIRES A REASON EVERYWHERE IN THIS CMS, NO EXCEPTIONS, and there is no toggle for it - the Leave and Expense toggles that used to make this optional have been deleted.
- THE MATRIX MUST HAVE A SEARCH BAR AND COLLAPSIBLE MODULE SECTIONS. 60+ screens in one flat list is not usable, and forcing Super Admin to expand all 13 modules just to find one toggle defeats the point of the search bar existing at all. Sections are collapsed by default; search covers both module names and screen names.
- ROLE ARCHITECTURE: exactly 5 roles exist (Super Admin, Employee, Manager, Accountant, HR), all treated as equally first-class. The Role Directory and the data model behind the Matrix must support a 6th role WITHOUT restructuring either screen - 'ready but not built', the same pattern used for Biometric Attendance and Timesheets. There is no 'Add Role' button or flow.
- ROLES ARE NOT CREATED OR DELETED FROM THE ROLE DIRECTORY. It has no buttons beyond navigation.
- EVERY ONE OF THE COMPILED TOGGLES MUST HAVE A HOME ON THE ROLE PERMISSION MATRIX, under its module's collapsible section, attached to the specific screen it affects.
- ONLY TWO TOGGLES IN THE WHOLE REGISTER DEFAULT TO ON: 'Require Manager Acknowledgment Before HR Processes Exit' (Employee) and 'Notify Employee When a Payslip Is Reissued' (Payroll). Every other active toggle defaults to OFF.
- THE COMPILED TOGGLE TABLE IS A SNAPSHOT, NOT INDEPENDENTLY AUTHORITATIVE over the module documents it was compiled from. If a module is revised with a new or renamed toggle, this register must be updated. Section 2.1 and Section 3 are the two places most likely to drift.
- TAXES ARE CONFIGURATION, NOT SPECIFICATION. No tax may be named in the spec: Masters -> Taxes is a single dynamic sub-master where each tax head is created by Super Admin (or Accountant with the toggle) and described by generic behaviour - scope, base, method, slabs, splits, ceilings, thresholds, relief, effective dates - never by name. Adding a tax must no longer change any document. Every rate or cap is a Masters field, never a number in a document.
- DO NOT BUILD: Labour Welfare Fund, Health & Education Cess, EDLI, ESOPs, Income Tax Slabs (TDS) sub-master, PF & ESI Rates sub-master, Professional Tax Slabs sub-master, Old Regime Deduction Limits sub-master, Team Leave Calendar, Team Payslips, Quote (Opportunity), or a Daily Task module. All were explicitly removed. Gratuity accrual and the wage-code validation (Basic + DA >= 50% of CTC on Salary Structure) were reviewed and KEPT.
- STATED COUNT DISCREPANCY TO RESOLVE AT BUILD TIME: Section 2.1 states 'That's 46 distinct toggles' and Assumption 5 states 43; the enumerated Section 2.1 table contains 49 rows (one of them the retired, struck-through 'Show Salary Amount to Manager'), plus the RBAC module's own 'Give HR Read-Only Access to the Permission Matrix' in Section 8. Build every enumerated toggle; treat the prose count as stale.

## 06 Payroll  (56 rules)

- PREVIEW LOCK (emphatic): The preview-before-confirm step in Run Payroll must be IMPOSSIBLE to skip. There is no path from selecting a Month/Year straight to generated payslips without passing through the Preview table first. Payroll mistakes are expensive and hard to undo, so nothing generates silently.
- HOURS ARE THE UNIT OF ACCOUNT (emphatic, Section 2.4): 'Hours are the unit of account here, not days. A day is only ever a display convenience; every figure that decides pay is an hour figure.'
- THE ATTENDANCE & TIME BASIS SCREEN IS LIVE AND IT DRIVES PAYROLL. Only the biometric feed is planned. The biometric feed is an INPUT METHOD, not a calculation, and nothing on the screen waits on it. An earlier revision wrongly marked the whole hour basis as 'Planned' — that was a stated error and must not be repeated.
- REQUIRED HOURS = Net Working Hours per Day × working days in the period, taken from whichever Work Week & Hours policy (Masters, Section 3.20) covers that employee. Working days = calendar weekdays − Mandatory holidays + whichever Saturdays that policy's Saturday rule makes working. Nothing in this calculation is a constant — a six-day support team and a half-day intern are measured against their own targets computed the same way.
- WORKED HOURS = (Check-Out − Check-In) − the unpaid break, summed across the period. Company standard: nine hours on site with a one-hour break, so a full day contributes 8.00. Nine hours on site with a two-hour lunch IS a shortfall and the screen must say so.
- LEAVE CREDIT = approved leave days × Net Working Hours per Day. Approved leave is credited, never treated as absence.
- COMPENSATORY CREDIT is an 'of which' SUBSET of Worked Hours, NEVER an addition to it — the same hours counted twice would silently overpay. The column is labelled 'of which Comp' and the reconciliation adds Worked + Leave ONLY.
- CREDITED = Worked + Leave Credit. BALANCE = Credited − Required. Negative Balance is a shortfall.
- SHORTFALL HANDLING: a negative balance is FIRST offset by any APPROVED compensatory hours. Whatever survives that is converted using the 'Uncompensated Shortfall Becomes' setting on the work-week policy — pro-rata per hour by default, which is the fairest of the available options and the one currently configured.
- PENDING COMP SUSPENDS, DOES NOT CANCEL: a pending compensatory request suspends the deduction. Approve the make-up day and the shortfall clears; reject it, or let the compensation window close, and the FULL amount converts at the next run. A partial make-up clears only its own hours, and the screen must state the remainder explicitly rather than implying the request fixes everything.
- NOTHING IS DEDUCTED SILENTLY: the employee sees the shortfall figure on Leave → Compensatory Hours BEFORE it reaches a payslip.
- HOUR STATUS BANDS: Met; 'Short — compensable' (shortfall within one day's net hours); 'Short — payroll impact' (beyond that).
- DAY-WISE BALANCE on the Day-wise Log tab is that day alone: Worked − Required. Day Type is resolved from the policy, so Sundays, non-working Saturdays and Mandatory holidays sit at ZERO required and cost nothing.
- GRACE WINDOW: a 15-minute grace window absorbs micro-shortfalls (worked example: 0.17 hours under on 24-06 kept the day a Full Day).
- HOUR BANDS resolve a day to Full Day / Half Day (worked example: 4.42 worked against 8.00 required resolved to Half Day).
- COMPENSATORY REQUEST VALIDATION (Leave, Section 2.6, validated against the employee's policy): must fall inside the 30-day compensation window; must stay under the 16-hour monthly cap; must be a day type the policy permits (a non-working 4th Saturday is permitted; a Mandatory holiday such as 15-08-2026 is REFUSED). The employee nominates the make-up day themselves.
- PAY CYCLE CUTOFF RULE (26th–25th default, Masters Section 3.20 Pay Cycle tab): Start Day 26, End Day 25. 'July 2026' covers 26-06-2026 through 25-07-2026; 'August 2026' covers 26-07-2026 through 25-08-2026.
- PAY CYCLE CARRYOVER WORKED EXAMPLE (must be implemented exactly): an employee takes one day of Approved Casual Leave on 27-07-2026. July's run window closes at 25-07-2026 — the 27th falls two days past the cutoff, so the leave day is NOT included in the July calculation or the July payslip. It is not lost: 27-07-2026 falls inside August's window (26-07 to 25-08) so it is picked up automatically when Run Payroll is used for August and appears on the AUGUST payslip. Nobody has to remember to move it or flag it manually — which period an event belongs to is decided purely by comparing its date against the Pay Cycle's resolved range for each month, the same comparison Run Payroll already does for every other dated input (attendance, compensatory make-up days, ad-hoc adjustments).
- SALARY CREDIT DATE: a separate, LATER date than the pay period's cutoff, resolved from the same Pay Cycle's Salary Credit Day. With the default cycle, 'July 2026' credits on 30-07-2026 — five days after the 25-07-2026 cutoff — leaving room for the run to be calculated and confirmed. It is shown on Run Payroll AND on the payslip header.
- PAY CYCLE IMMUTABILITY: changing the Pay Cycle changes Pay Period Covers for every FUTURE run; it NEVER rewrites a period already run.
- UNPAID LEAVE DEDUCTION = (Gross Pay ÷ Total Days in Month) × Unpaid Leave Days.
- PAID DAYS = days present + paid leave. Approved leave counts as present — 'Taken' in the Leave module already means every Approved request regardless of date, so this reads directly from that.
- HOLIDAYS AND WEEKENDS are non-working, PAID days — never deducted, whether or not the employee was 'present.'
- STATUTORY HEAD APPLICATION ORDER (exact sequence): for each applicable head, the amount is whatever the head's own configuration produces — a percentage of its configured base, a fixed amount, or a slab lookup — after applying its Minimum Base, then Maximum Base/Ceiling, then Cut-Off Base, then per-period cap, then rounding rule, IN THAT ORDER.
- STATUTORY APPLICABILITY is decided by the head itself: its Applies From/Until dates, its Applicable Locations and Employment Types, the employee's tax scheme if schemes are in use, and the per-employee Applicable toggle on their Salary Structure where the head permits one.
- EMPLOYEE-BORNE amounts reduce Net Pay. COMPANY-BORNE amounts do NOT reduce Net Pay and instead feed Annual CTC where the head sets Include in Cost to Company.
- NO KNOWLEDGE OF SPECIFIC DEDUCTIONS (emphatic): 'This module contains no knowledge of any specific deduction. It does not know what PF, ESI, or Professional Tax are; it reads heads and applies them.'
- INCOME TAX METHOD, in order — (a) ANNUALIZE: Gross Monthly × 12, plus any taxable ad-hoc additions already paid this year, plus any annualized variable pay. (b) SUBTRACT every reduction the configuration allows: each Submitted declaration from My Tax Declarations whose head has 'Reduces Taxable Income' = Yes, capped at that head's Maximum Amount per Period; plus any monthly statutory deduction whose head also has 'Reduces Taxable Income' = Yes, annualized. Nothing is subtracted unless a head says it may be — there is NO built-in standard deduction and NO built-in list of what's deductible. (c) APPLY the head's calculation: its slab bands using its configured Slab Application (progressive, or flat by band), or its flat rate, whichever it is set to. (d) APPLY the head's Relief Rules if it has any — each rule that triggers reduces the computed tax by the LESSER of its Relief Amount or the tax itself, so relief can bring tax to zero but NEVER below it. (e) DIVIDE by the remaining payroll months in the financial year to get this month's amount, recalculated automatically whenever Gross, declarations, or scheme choice change partway through the year.
- IF NO INCOME-TAX HEAD IS CONFIGURED this step produces nothing and is SKIPPED ENTIRELY — no error, no zero-line on the payslip. That is the correct behaviour for a company that hasn't told the system it withholds income tax.
- KNOWN CALCULATION GAP: the design cannot express a taper above a relief threshold (marginal relief). Relief Rules handle the threshold itself but not the taper above it. If a configured head needs that behaviour it is a real addition to make before build — the one calculation shape the current design cannot express.
- ALREADY-PROCESSED SKIP: per employee + Month/Year. An employee with a Processed run for the selected period is auto-excluded from a new run for that period, whether running 'All active employees' or explicitly selecting them, so nobody gets double-processed. A DRAFT run does not trigger the skip; only Processed does.
- CONFIGURATION SNAPSHOT (emphatic): 'A processed payslip records the configuration that produced it, not just the result.' Each calculated statutory line stores which tax head it came from and what that head's figures were at the moment of calculation — this is what makes a payslip from two years ago still explainable after the head has been retired and superseded, and it is why tax heads LOCK once used (Masters, Section 3.2, Status & Deletion Rule) rather than being edited in place.
- PREVIEW DEDUCTION COLUMNS ARE BUILT FROM CONFIGURATION: a run with three configured heads shows three columns; a run with none shows only Unpaid Leave.
- STATUS TRANSITIONS: (new) → Draft (Save as Draft: recalculable, discardable, no payslips) → Processed (Confirm & Generate Payslips: payslips generated, employees notified, locked) → Reissued (via Reissue only, with required Reason and a Reissue History entry). A Draft may also be confirmed directly without ever being saved as Draft. Processed is never edited in place.
- AD-HOC ADJUSTMENT LIFECYCLE: a one-time adjustment included in a run is SPENT — it does not carry forward. A Recurring adjustment remains queued for the next run until removed. Removing a Recurring adjustment stops it from FUTURE runs only; it does not undo it from runs already Processed — correcting a past Processed payslip goes through Reissue (2.3), not the adjustments screen.
- AD-HOC AMOUNT must be a positive number; the Type (Addition / Subtraction) determines whether it adds to or subtracts from pay. Additions land on Gross Pay or Net Pay depending on how each one was configured, applied BEFORE the Preview table is shown.
- AD-HOC FROM APPOINTMENT LETTER: a Custom Term saved on the Offer & Appointment Letter screen (Employee module, Section 2.16) using the same Label/Type/Amount shape is queued into Ad-hoc Adjustments AUTOMATICALLY, effective from the employee's Joining Date — no manual re-entry once they've joined and payroll starts running for them.
- HIKES ARE NOT AD-HOC: a permanent salary increase is a NEW Salary Structure entry with a new Effective From date (Employee module, Section 2.2), tracked in Structure Change History. The Ad-hoc screen is for one-time or short-term items only, never for changing ongoing fixed pay.
- AD-HOC TRANSPARENCY DEFAULT: the Label and Amount appear on the employee's own payslip breakup exactly like any other line item, with the ACTUAL Label ('Diwali Bonus: +₹5,000', 'Laptop Advance Recovery: −₹2,000') rather than a generic 'Adjustment' — deliberately transparent by default; a number appearing with no explanation would raise more questions than it answers.
- EMPLOYEE PAYSLIP LIST shows ONLY Processed months. Nothing shows for a month still in Draft or not yet run.
- SHOW ON PAYSLIP = NO: heads with that setting are CALCULATED but NOT PRINTED — the setting exists for accrued employer-side items an employee has no reason to see as a deduction.
- PAYSLIP PRINT LABELS: each employee-borne tax head prints under its own Print Label from Masters.
- REISSUE REQUIRES A REASON: a Reason (text, required) must be given before a reissue can be confirmed, on BOTH the recalculate path and the manual-adjust path. On confirm, the payslip updates and a Reissue History entry is recorded on it — Date, Reissued By, Reason, and the OLD figures alongside the NEW ones — the same accountability pattern used for asset assignment history, ticket assignment history and leave balance adjustment history elsewhere in this CMS.
- EXPORT MIRRORS THE SCREEN EXACTLY (emphatic): 'The one place amounts are restricted is the column, not the export button.' Export follows the filter and follows the column. There is no second rule and no separate export permission.
- ACCOUNTANT/HR EXPORT HEADER: exports carry a header block stating the filters applied — 'on a payroll register this is not optional politeness. An exported spreadsheet of 12 employees that doesn't say it was filtered to one department will be read as the whole company.'
- SALARY CERTIFICATE is generated ON DEMAND and is NOT stored as a monthly record the way a payslip is, since it can be requested at any time. It pulls from the Employee record (Name, Designation, Joining Date), Company Profile (official company details and letterhead — Masters 3.6a/e), and the current Salary Structure.
- PER-EMPLOYEE APPLICABILITY: a head marked as permitting per-employee opt-out gets an Applicable toggle on that employee's Salary Structure, editable by Super Admin or Accountant. If set to No, this module skips that head ENTIRELY for that person — deduction and matching employer contribution — and the same setting is what the Offer & Appointment Letter discloses, so nobody gets a letter promising a deduction that doesn't apply, or vice versa.
- WORKING DAYS IN MONTH is auto-calculated (calendar minus weekends minus Mandatory holidays from Masters → Holidays) but is SHOWN and OVERRIDABLE — e.g. for a special company-wide day off never formally added as a Holiday, or a regional variation between the Chennai and Coimbatore offices.
- STATUS TAG COLOURS (assumption, approved): Draft = grey, Processed = green.
- PAID vs UNPAID LEAVE VISUAL RULE: wherever a day-basis breakdown is shown, 'Paid Leave (counted as present)' and 'Unpaid Leave (deducted)' need a CLEAR visual difference — distinct styling / colour-coding, not just two rows that look the same — since confusing the two directly affects someone's pay.
- OFFICIAL DOCUMENT RULE: Payslip and Salary Certificate downloads must read like an official document — proper letterhead, company details and formatting from Company Profile — since employees may need to submit them to a bank, embassy or landlord.
- NO NAMED TAX, EVER (emphatic closing guarantee): 'it calculates nothing on its own authority. Every statutory figure on every payslip traces to a tax head someone deliberately created, and this document contains no rate, no slab, no threshold, and no tax name.' What this module must never reacquire: a named tax, a rate, a slab, or a threshold.
- TEAM PAYSLIPS / 'Payslip — Manager View' IS REMOVED ENTIRELY on explicit instruction, from BOTH this module and the Employee module. A Manager gets NO payslip-related visibility into their team at all — not even a status-only Processed/Pending view. Do not build it. If a team-level pay view is ever wanted back it should be designed once, in one module, rather than reintroduced as a duplicate in both.
- BIOMETRIC FUTURE-PROOFING: when a device or external system starts writing check-in/check-out times, nothing downstream changes. The stored record keeps its shape; only 'Recorded By' changes from a person to a device or system source — which is exactly why that field was never a strict user reference. Required Hours, Worked Hours, the hour bands, compensation and the LOP conversion all keep working because they were never reading the input method, only the times.
- SIDEBAR PLACEMENT (changed 2026-07-30): Payroll and Leave (Module 5) share ONE left-hand sidebar tab, 'Leave & Payroll', instead of two separate ones. My Tax Declarations (Employee, Module 2) moved into the same tab since it feeds the income-tax calculation. Screens, fields and access rules are unchanged — only navigation position. See RBAC Module 14, Section 3.

## 05 Leave  (48 rules)

- REMAINING FORMULA: Remaining = Allowed + Carried Forward − Taken − Pending. All five columns appear identically on My Leave (2.1) and Leave Balance Management (2.3).
- ZERO-QUOTA TYPES: If a Leave Type's Default Annual Quota in Masters is 0 (permitted for types like WFH that don't consume a quota), Remaining displays the literal text 'Not tracked' instead of a number, and the hard balance check does NOT apply to that type.
- TAKEN DEFINITION: Taken = days from EVERY Approved request for that Leave Type, whether the dates are in the past or still upcoming. There is no separate 'approved but upcoming' bucket.
- DAYS-REQUESTED FORMULA: days requested = working days between From Date and To Date, EXCLUDING weekly off days and MANDATORY holidays from the Company Holiday Calendar (Optional holidays do NOT reduce the count), MINUS 0.5 if Half-Day = Yes.
- SATURDAY RULE: which Saturdays are working is NOT hardcoded — it resolves from the Saturday rule on the applicable work-week policy (Masters 3.20), currently ODD Saturdays working and EVEN Saturdays off. A MANDATORY holiday falling on a working Saturday makes that Saturday non-working; an OPTIONAL holiday does not.
- HARD BLOCK ON INSUFFICIENT BALANCE: if days requested > Remaining for the selected Leave Type, SUBMISSION IS BLOCKED OUTRIGHT. There is NO submit-anyway option, no override, no 'approve as an exception' path. A request that fails this check simply cannot be created. This is a hard rule, not a warning. The remedy for genuinely needing more days is an HR balance adjustment first (2.3), never an override at the point of applying.
- BLOCKED MESSAGE WORDING: the blocked-by-balance message must state the shortfall EXACTLY — e.g. 'You have 2 days remaining for Casual Leave, but requested 4' — NEVER a generic 'insufficient balance' error.
- OVERLAP BLOCK: a second, separate check blocks any request whose dates overlap an existing Pending or Approved request from the SAME person.
- TO DATE >= FROM DATE: To Date must be on or after From Date.
- HALF-DAY: setting Half-Day = Yes reveals a mandatory First Half / Second Half choice and subtracts 0.5 from the day count.
- STATUS TRANSITIONS (Leave Request): created at PENDING → APPROVED (days move from Pending into Taken; Remaining reflects the new total; applicant notified) OR → REJECTED (Remaining unaffected — the days were only ever held in Pending; reason required; applicant notified with the reason) OR → CANCELLED (only by the applicant, only while still Pending; days return to Remaining immediately).
- CANCEL SCOPE: the 'Cancel Request' button is shown ONLY on the applicant's OWN still-Pending requests.
- REJECTION REASON MANDATORY: Reject requires a reason on both Leave Approvals (2.2) and Compensatory Hours (2.6). Flipped from optional to required in this revision; the toggle that would restore optional behaviour has been deleted.
- ROUTING PRECEDENCE (exact, applied in order): (1) applicant's Reporting Manager — the specific named person on their Employee record's Employment Details tab, not the Manager role as a group; (2) HR if no Reporting Manager is set; (3) HR ALWAYS if the applicant is themselves a Manager, Accountant, or HR person, REGARDLESS of whether they have a Reporting Manager on file; (4) Super Admin if there is no active HR user; (5) Super Admin if the request would route to HR but the applicant IS the only active HR user (self-approval fallthrough); (6) HR's STANDING OVERRIDE — HR may approve, reject, or REASSIGN any request at any time regardless of where it is currently routed; this is permanent, not conditional.
- NO SELF-APPROVAL: a person never approves their own request, in any role, under any routing outcome.
- HR VISIBILITY: a request appears in HR's company-wide Leave Approvals queue REGARDLESS of who it is routed to, since HR can act on anything. The 'Routed To' column still shows whose turn it normally is.
- APPROVER NOTIFICATION: the designated approver is notified IMMEDIATELY when the request is created.
- NEW-EMPLOYEE PRORATION FORMULA (authoritative here): Allowed = (Default Annual Quota ÷ 12) × months remaining in the financial year from the Joining Date, ROUNDED TO THE NEAREST HALF-DAY. Applied AUTOMATICALLY to every ACTIVE Leave Type the moment an Employee record is first saved as Active within a financial year. This is not a button and requires no manual step. Carried Forward starts at 0 for a new employee.
- CARRY-FORWARD FORMULA: for each employee and each Leave Type where Masters' Carry Forward Allowed = Yes, carry over that employee's REMAINING balance for that type, CAPPED at that type's Max Carry Forward Days. Anything above the cap is NOT carried forward and is dropped.
- CARRY-FORWARD IS MANUAL AND ONE-TIME: a deliberate, per-financial-year action triggered by button, never recalculated silently in the background. It must show a per-employee, per-type PREVIEW before committing, followed by a confirm-before-you-go prompt, because running it twice or on the wrong financial year is hard to undo cleanly.
- CARRIED FORWARD IS NEVER TYPED: it is a computed field, in the same way depreciation in Asset Management is computed, not typed.
- BALANCE ADJUSTMENT: amount may be positive or negative; a Reason/Note is REQUIRED on EVERY adjustment; the balance updates immediately; every adjustment is logged to Adjustment History with Date, Leave Type, Amount, Reason and Adjusted By; the employee is notified with the reason.
- LEAVE LIABILITY FORMULA: total unused leave across every ACTIVE employee = sum of (Remaining + Carried Forward), expressed in days. Displayed as a single prominent headline figure, not buried in a table.
- REPORT FILTER COHERENCE: all filters on Leave Reports apply across all four report views SIMULTANEOUSLY — switching from 'by Type' to 'Leave Liability' must never silently change the underlying set. A liability figure quietly reflecting a different filter than the table above it would be worse than no figure at all.
- REPORT EXPORT HEADER BLOCK: every export from Leave Reports carries a header block stating the filters that produced it, the result count, who exported it, and when — because a leave-liability number without its date range and status filter attached will be read as a company-wide total.
- EXPORT FOLLOWS THE FILTER on every screen in this module, per the Filter & Export Convention (RBAC Module 14, Section 2.2).
- COMPENSATORY HOURS — SHORTFALL CANNOT BE INVENTED: the Shortfall Date dropdown offers ONLY dates that genuinely recorded a shortfall in attendance.
- COMPENSATORY HOURS — HOURS SHORT FORMULA: Hours Short = Required Hours − Net Worked Hours for that date. It is derived, read-only, and is THE CEILING on what may be claimed.
- COMPENSATORY HOURS — MINIMUM MAKE-UP: 'Hours You Will Work' must be AT LEAST Hours Short. Anything beyond simply closes the shortfall; the excess is NOT banked as extra credit.
- COMPENSATORY HOURS — MAKE-UP DATE IS THE EMPLOYEE'S OWN CHOICE, validated LIVE on submit against three constraints: (a) inside the Compensation Window on their work-week policy — a date past the window is REFUSED OUTRIGHT with the last permissible date stated; (b) under Max Compensatory Hours per Month on that policy; (c) on a day type the policy permits — a non-working Saturday IS usually allowed, a MANDATORY HOLIDAY IS NOT.
- COMPENSATORY HOURS — BREAK RULE: a break is only deducted from the planned stretch if it runs PAST SIX HOURS.
- COMPENSATORY HOURS — STATUS TRANSITIONS: created at PENDING → APPROVED (hours credited against that SPECIFIC shortfall, the day lifts back to Full Day, nothing reaches the payslip) OR → REJECTED (reason required; shortfall converts to Loss of Pay at the next payroll run, per the policy's 'Uncompensated Shortfall Becomes' setting).
- COMPENSATORY HOURS — PENDING SUSPENDS, DOES NOT CANCEL: while Pending, Payroll SUSPENDS the deduction rather than cancelling it; the shortfall remains visible on Payroll → Attendance's Payroll Impact tab with the pending hours shown against it.
- COMPENSATORY HOURS — WINDOW EXPIRY: if the compensation window closes before approval, the shortfall converts to Loss of Pay exactly as on a rejection.
- COMPENSATORY HOURS — PARTIAL MAKE-UP: clears ONLY its own hours. The remainder still deducts, and BOTH the Leave screen and the Payroll screen must state the split EXPLICITLY rather than implying the request resolved everything.
- COMPENSATORY HOURS ≠ COMP OFF: Comp Off is a whole DAY of leave EARNED by working a day off and drawn later from a balance; Compensatory Hours is HOURS OWED against a specific dated shortfall and worked afterwards, usually in the same period. Both exist; conflating them would let a two-hour make-up bank a full day of leave.
- COMPENSATORY HOURS — PAYROLL IMPACT SHOWN UP FRONT: the currency 'Payroll Impact if Rejected' is displayed on the request form before submission, so nobody is surprised by their payslip.
- COMPENSATORY HOURS REUSES THE LEAVE ROUTING RULE (2.2/6.1) rather than inventing a second one.
- HOLIDAY CALENDAR IS READ-ONLY IN THIS MODULE: it is sourced entirely from Masters (Section 3.5) and is not editable from here at all; editing happens in Masters by Super Admin and Manager per the Masters module.
- BALANCE PANEL IS THE FIRST THING ANYONE SEES on this module — a small set of numbers per Leave Type, front and centre, not a settings page and not a table you have to scroll to find.
- LEAVE TYPE FILTER IS STRUCTURAL on Leave Balance Management: the table shows ONE Leave Type at a time, because every type for every employee in one flat table would be unreadable.
- STATUS TAG COLOURS (everywhere a request appears): Pending = amber, Approved = green, Rejected = red, Cancelled = grey.
- MANDATORY vs OPTIONAL HOLIDAY BADGES: solid tag for Mandatory, outlined tag for Optional.
- BASELINE SELF-SERVICE FLOOR: nobody's access to their OWN leave (and their own Compensatory Hours) may ever be narrower than a plain Employee's, in any role — including Accountant, whose source-spreadsheet row covered only the admin/approval side.
- NOTHING IN THIS MODULE'S ACCESS IS HARDCODED — all of it is driven from the central RBAC settings screen (Roles & Access, Module 14), including per-person overrides granted by Super Admin without changing the role-wide setting.
- SIDEBAR PLACEMENT (changed 2026-07-30): Leave and Payroll share ONE left-hand sidebar tab called 'Leave & Payroll'. Navigation only — no screen, field, or access rule changes.
- THIS MODULE HAS NOTHING TO DO WITH Meshrd's retail hardware products — it only tracks internal staff leave.
- EMPTY STATES (exact wording): Company Holiday Calendar — 'No holidays set up for this financial year yet.'; Leave Reports — 'No leave data for this date range.'; Compensatory Hours — 'No compensatory hour requests.'

Paid Into is shown on the usage row rather than only on the vendor record SO THAT A HISTORIC PAYMENT STILL SHOWS WHERE IT ACTUALLY WENT even after the vendor later changes their account details — the name recorded against a past payment is the evidence that it went to the right party at the time. Implication: snapshot the account name/number onto each payment, do not resolve it live from the current vendor record.
- VENDOR USAGE — BOTH ACCOUNT COLUMNS ARE EMPTY ON A ROW THAT HAS NOT BEEN PAID, AND THAT IS THE POINT: an Unpaid bill with no account is CORRECT; a PAID one without an account is A GAP WORTH SEEING. Do not hide or backfill blanks.
- VENDOR USAGE — NO TOTALS AND NO CHARTS, EVER. It stays a list of linked records, not a spend report; spend reporting belongs to Purchase Bill and Expense. Filters and export were added; totals and charts were deliberately withheld.
- VENDOR USAGE — ordering: one row per linked record, MOST RECENT FIRST.
- VENDOR — FIELD VISIBILITY DECIDES FILTER VISIBILITY: where a field is hidden from a role, any filter reading that field is hidden from that role too. Applied concretely: with 'Allow Manager to Edit Vendor Bank & GST Details' OFF, Manager loses the bank/GST fields AND the 'has bank details on file' filter.
- VENDOR — EXPORT CARRIES THE SCREEN'S COLUMN VISIBILITY: a Manager's export contains no bank or GST columns when those are hidden. Export always reflects the filtered set.
- VENDOR — NO auto-generated code of any kind (no Customer-Code / Asset-Tag equivalent), consistent with having no Masters dependency.
- VENDOR — KEEP THIS MODULE SIMPLE: a list and a form, nothing more. No dashboard, no reports screen — that level of detail belongs to Purchase Bill and Expense, where the money actually moves.
- PURCHASE BILL — TWO DIFFERENT BILL NUMBERS, KEPT CLEARLY SEPARATE (stated plainly because they are easy to conflate). (a) INTERNAL PURCHASE BILL ID: auto-generated ON SAVE, format PB-<Financial Year>-<4-digit number>, e.g. PB-2026-0001, using the prefix from Company Profile in Masters (Section 3.6e) — the same pattern used for asset tags and ticket numbers. It is Meshrd's own reference and is NEVER SEEN BY THE VENDOR. (b) VENDOR BILL NUMBER: manually entered, REQUIRED, the vendor's OWN invoice/bill number printed on their document, needed to match this record back to the physical or PDF bill later. The two have nothing to do with each other and must both be LABELLED CLEARLY ON SCREEN so they are never confused.
- PURCHASE BILL — Bill Date CANNOT BE IN THE FUTURE.
- PURCHASE BILL — Due Date MUST BE AFTER Bill Date if provided.
- PURCHASE BILL — Line Total = Quantity × Rate (read-only, computed). Quantity must be a positive number. Rate must be a positive number.
- PURCHASE BILL — Subtotal = SUM of every Line Total (read-only, computed).
- PURCHASE BILL — Total = Subtotal + Tax Amount (read-only, computed).
- PURCHASE BILL — TAX AMOUNT, TWO ROUTES, OVERRIDE ALWAYS AVAILABLE: either pick a head from Masters → Taxes (Section 3.2, PURCHASE scope, in force ON THE BILL DATE) which auto-fills the figure, OR type the exact amount directly. Because the bill already exists as a real document, the vendor's own tax figure may not land perfectly on a configured head (rounding, for instance), so A DIRECT OVERRIDE STAYS AVAILABLE EVEN AFTER PICKING ONE. Direct entry also means a bill can be recorded correctly BEFORE ANY TAX HEAD HAS BEEN CONFIGURED AT ALL. No tax may be hardcoded or named — Masters Taxes is a dynamic list Super Admin populates.
- PURCHASE BILL — STATUS SEQUENCE: Unpaid → Part-Paid → Paid. A new bill starts DIRECTLY at Unpaid. THERE IS NO DRAFT AND NO SENT STAGE AT ALL, because a Purchase Bill records something that already happened rather than a document being composed.
- PURCHASE BILL — LIVE RECALCULATION: Line Total, Subtotal, Tax Amount and Total all update IMMEDIATELY as figures are typed, no manual refresh — the same pattern used on Invoice.
- RECORD PAYMENT — Amount must be POSITIVE and CANNOT EXCEED Balance Due; overpayment is BLOCKED OUTRIGHT.
- RECORD PAYMENT — ON SUBMIT: the balance updates automatically; if the new TOTAL PAID EQUALS the bill's Total, Status moves to PAID; if it is MORE THAN ZERO BUT LESS THAN the Total, Status moves to PART-PAID. MULTIPLE PARTIAL PAYMENTS are supported before a bill reaches Paid.
- RECORD PAYMENT — availability lock: only on a bill with Status Unpaid or Part-Paid, NEVER on one already Paid.
- RECORD PAYMENT — Paid From (Meshrd Account) DEFAULTS to the account named on the bill (PB-2.2) and STAYS EDITABLE, because a bill can legitimately be settled from a different account than the one it was filed against.
- RECORD PAYMENT — Paid Into (Vendor's Account) is READ-ONLY ON PURPOSE: 'a payment screen is the wrong place to change a supplier's account details, and letting it be edited inline is exactly how a payment ends up going somewhere nobody approved.' Never allow inline edit of vendor bank details from a payment screen.
- PURCHASE BILL LIST — the BANK ACCOUNT COLUMN carries the account NAMED ON THE BILL (PB-2.2), or the account a payment was ACTUALLY MADE FROM once one is recorded (PB-2.3) — the latter is the figure that matters for reconciliation. The same column exists on the Invoice list (Module 11, Section 2.2) and on both Expense list screens (Module 12), so payables, receivables and claims can all be read account by account.
- PURCHASE BILL LIST — OVERDUE = Status is NOT Paid AND Due Date has passed.
- PURCHASE BILL LIST — AGEING BUCKETS: Current / 1–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ days overdue. Ageing matters as much on the payables side as on receivables, and arguably more — a supplier bill quietly passing its due date damages a relationship the company depends on, a slower and less visible failure than a customer paying late. This list is worked BY HOW OVERDUE SOMETHING IS, not by supplier name.
- PURCHASE BILL — EXPORT covers EXACTLY the filtered, searched, sorted set, with a HEADER BLOCK naming the filters, the result count, who exported it, and when.
- PURCHASE BILL — NO PDF-GENERATION 'DOWNLOAD' BUTTON, unlike Invoice. There is no one to send a Purchase Bill to; it's an internal record of something already received. What matters is opening the ATTACHMENT quickly.
- PURCHASE BILL — ATTACHMENT PROMINENCE: the attachment is the ACTUAL SOURCE DOCUMENT FOR AUDIT PURPOSES, not a nice-to-have tucked away behind a small icon. Show a thumbnail preview, similar to how receipts are shown in the Expense module.
- PURCHASE BILL — STATUS TAG COLORS (convention): Unpaid = amber, Part-Paid = blue, Paid = green.
- LINKED EXPENSE CLAIM — the same piece of spending can reach the system twice (supplier's bill here + staff claim in Expense because an employee paid on a personal card; the office Wi-Fi bill is the standard example) and neither record reveals on its own that they are the same money. LINKED, the two SETTLE AS A SINGLE AMOUNT and each record SHOWS THE OTHER on its own record. UNLINKED, the company pays the supplier AND reimburses the employee, and the duplication surfaces only at reconciliation, if at all. The link is DELIBERATELY OPTIONAL AND TWO-WAY: it can be made from the Expense side (Module 12, Section 2.1) and EITHER DIRECTION POPULATES BOTH RECORDS. Mandatory would force a meaningless 'None' on the majority of bills.
- PURCHASE BILL — SEQUENCING PREREQUISITE: suppliers (Vendors) MUST ALREADY EXIST before a bill can be recorded against one; the inline '+ Add New Vendor' modal is the sanctioned way to satisfy that without leaving the bill form.
- DERIVED-VALUE STANDING RULE (applies to both modules): every derived/computed value — Internal Purchase Bill ID, Line Total, Subtotal, Total, Paid, Balance Due, Status, Financial Year, Created On/By, Last Modified By, and every Vendor Usage column — is rendered READ-ONLY and IS NEVER TYPED. The one deliberate exception is Tax Amount, which may be auto-filled from a tax head and STILL typed over.

## 15 Tax & Compliance  (18 rules)

- A head enters the Module 15 register ONLY when `Creates an Onward Liability` = Yes. This gate is valid on EVERY scope, which is how a payroll head joins the same register as a supplier withholding without duplicating machinery.
- If no tax head anywhere has the gate on, the entire Tax & Compliance module is hidden - no sidebar tab, no screens.
- Three sources feed one register: Purchase Bill (source = VENDOR), Payroll (source = EMPLOYEE), Invoice Record Payment (source = CUSTOMER, the credit side).
- The payable register (liability) and the receivable register (asset) are NEVER netted into a single figure. A 'net tax position' number is wrong in both directions.
- Obligation period boundaries come from the head's `Period Aligns To`: Calendar / Financial Year / Pay Cycle. A PAYROLL head must use Pay Cycle (26th-25th, Masters 3.20c) - calendar alignment would disagree with the payslips that produced it.
- Due Date = period end + the head's `Deposit Due - Days After Period End`. Expressed in days, never a fixed calendar day, so short months need no second rule.
- Obligation status precedence, first match wins: Remitted (remitted = withheld) -> Overdue (due date passed, outstanding > 0) -> Due Soon (due within 7 days) -> Part-Remitted (0 < remitted < withheld) -> Open. A partly-remitted overdue period reads Overdue.
- The 7-day Due Soon threshold is a fixed platform figure driving a badge colour, not a per-head field - same footing as the existing ageing buckets.
- `Liability Arises On` (Source Document Date / Payment Date) decides which period an amount belongs to, and LOCKS once the first remittance exists under that head. Changing it later would silently re-file every historical amount.
- A remittance is IMMUTABLE after save. Corrections are a negative-signed reversal referencing the original - never an edit or delete. Reversal is Super Admin only and requires a reason.
- `Documents Covered` on a remittance is confirmed by the operator, NEVER inferred. No FIFO or automatic allocation. Part-allocation on the final ticked document is permitted so an odd remittance figure can never force an untrue allocation.
- The ticked Documents Covered total must equal Amount Remitted.
- Where a withholding certificate states a different figure than the receipts it covers, the CERTIFIED amount is what may be claimed - not the company's recorded figure. The authority accepts the certificate. Variance is surfaced, never auto-corrected in either direction.
- Only a certified receipt may be marked Credit Claimed. An uncertified receipt has no evidence behind it.
- Certificate Reference is unique per customer.
- Voiding a certificate is Super Admin only and requires a reason.
- Tax & Compliance is Accountant + Super Admin only. Manager, HR and Employee have no access and the module does not appear in their navigation. An employee's own deducted tax reaches them on their payslip, not here.
- OUT OF SCOPE, must not be assumed to exist: interest/penalty computation on a late remittance; filing returns or integrating with any authority's systems; employee-facing annual tax certificates (an obligation to ISSUE, belonging with Payroll document generation); the company's own income-tax liability.
